Privacy Policy - Cosmic Escape

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cosmic Escape mobile application (the "Application") operated by Kandarp Pandya (the "Service Provider"). It explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Application.

Effective date: October 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Cosmic Escape does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or email address in order to play the game.

If you voluntarily contact the Service Provider by email, the Service Provider will receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

The Application and the third-party services integrated into it may automatically process certain technical, usage, advertising, and diagnostic information. Depending on the service and your privacy choices, this may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate/general location inferred from it;
  • device and operating system information;
  • app version, package name, language, country, time zone, and similar configuration information;
  • device or app-instance identifiers used by Google services;
  • gameplay and app interaction events;
  • advertising interactions and ad-delivery information;
  • crash reports, stack traces, diagnostics, and related app/device state.

2. Gameplay and Analytics Information

Cosmic Escape uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Application is used and to improve gameplay, stability, and user experience.

Analytics events may include information such as:

  • application sessions;
  • chapter and level starts and completions;
  • level restarts and deadlock events;
  • Hint requests and results;
  • chapter and campaign progress;
  • advertising lifecycle events, such as whether an ad was requested, available, shown, or completed.

These analytics events are intended to describe use of the Application and are not designed to include information you type into forms, private messages, or account profile information because Cosmic Escape does not provide those features in its current version.

3. Advertising

Cosmic Escape uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising. The Application currently uses:

  • rewarded ads, including ads that may be offered in connection with the Hint feature; and
  • interstitial ads shown at eligible points in the game.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes including ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

A Hint is granted only when the rewarded-ad flow reports that the reward has been earned. If an ad is unavailable, fails to load, or cannot be shown, the Application does not provide a free rewarded Hint as a substitute.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Cosmic Escape uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and related Google privacy tools to request and manage advertising consent or privacy choices.

Advertising requests are made only when Google's consent system indicates that ads may be requested. Where Google requires privacy options to remain available, the Application may provide a Privacy Choices option in Settings so that you can review or update your choices.

Your available choices may vary depending on your location and applicable law.

5. Firebase Crashlytics

Cosmic Escape uses Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes, non-fatal errors, and stability issues. Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, diagnostic information, app state, device metadata, installation identifiers, and related technical information needed to diagnose application problems.

6. Firebase Remote Config

Cosmic Escape uses Firebase Remote Config to manage operational configuration without requiring a new application release. For example, Remote Config may be used to enable or disable advertising features and to provide advertising configuration such as ad-unit identifiers.

Remote Config is not used by Cosmic Escape to deliver new production levels or to store passwords, private keys, payment information, or other secrets.

Firebase Remote Config may process technical information such as country, language, time zone, platform and operating system version, Firebase application identifier, package name, and SDK version.

7. Local Game Data

Cosmic Escape stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, which may include:

  • game progress;
  • completed and unlocked levels;
  • tutorial or onboarding state;
  • game settings and similar preferences.

Local game data is generally removed when you clear the Application's storage or uninstall the Application, subject to the behavior of your device operating system and any backup or restore features you have enabled.

8. Third-Party Services

Cosmic Escape uses third-party services provided by Google. These services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

9. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the Application and its integrated services may be used to:

  • provide and operate the game;
  • save and restore gameplay state on the device;
  • understand gameplay usage and improve the user experience;
  • detect and diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • deliver and measure advertising;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Data Sharing

The Service Provider does not sell personal information directly.

Information may be processed by Google and Firebase services used by the Application, including for analytics, crash reporting, advertising, consent management, configuration, security, and fraud prevention, according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the Service Provider, or others.

11. International Processing

Google, Firebase, and other service providers used by the Application may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Such processing is governed by the applicable service provider's terms, privacy policy, and legal safeguards.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is changed or removed by the Application, cleared through your device settings, removed when the Application is uninstalled, or otherwise affected by device backup and restore features.

Information processed by Google, Firebase, AdMob, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, or consent services is retained according to the applicable service settings, retention controls, and privacy policies of those providers.

If you contact the Service Provider directly, correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain appropriate records, or comply with legal obligations.
